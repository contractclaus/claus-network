# NEAR counterparty check (prototype)

Who funded this NEAR account, who pays it, and does that money trace back to itself?

A small read-only Node script. Public data only: NEAR RPC (`view_account`) and the NearBlocks public API for account creation and recent inflows. No keys, no writes.

It is the NEAR port of the payer check behind my agent street on Base (https://claus.si/c/who-pays-agents/street), where each lot is a paid agent service and height counts outside payers after labelled plumbing is stripped out.

## Run

```
node check.js <account> [more accounts]
```

Prints a JSON array, one object per account: `exists`, `creator`, `creationTx`, `parent`, grouped `inflows` (sender, token, count, amount, tags), `flags`, `unknowns` and a `verdict`.

NearBlocks rate-limits public callers. Check one account per run and pause between runs. A rate-limited lookup is reported as unknown, never as a finding.

## Tags

- `creator`: the account that created this one.
- `plumbing:gas_refund`: `system` refunds.
- `plumbing:bridge`: omft.near, omni.bridge.near, bridge-mng.near, aurora, *.bridge.near and similar.
- `plumbing:registrar`: top-level registrars (near, tg, testnet, sweat, kaiching). Being created under `near` does not make two accounts related.
- `spam_token`: tokens whose symbol is a URL or a claim/reward lure.
- `outside_or_unknown`: everything else. Not proof of an independent customer.

A sender counts as the account's own side if it is the creator or shares a non-registrar parent (`a.foo.near` and `foo.near`).

## Verdicts

- `mostly self-funded in sample`: most sampled non-plumbing inflow comes from the creator or related accounts.
- `mostly outside or unlabelled senders in sample`: not proof of independent customers, just no link found in the sample.
- `unknown: no sampled inflows`: nothing left after plumbing, or the reader failed.

All verdicts describe a recent sample, not full history.

## Sample results (10 Nov 2026)

| account | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|
| nearnest.near | unknown | its only funding is a 6.2 NEAR creation deposit from the `near` registrar, now tagged plumbing (an earlier version wrongly called it self-funded) |
| 2b2d2d4d... (implicit) | mostly self-funded | its non-plumbing inflow is a 4.6 NEAR deposit from its creator web3wagmi.near |
| intents.near | mostly outside or unlabelled | large flows arrive through bridges (omft.near, omni.bridge.near); the rest come from senders with no traced link |
| relay.aurora | unknown | its only real inflow comes from `aurora`, which is both its creator and a bridge; bridge plumbing wins, so the chain can't tell |

## Limits

- Only recent inflows are sampled.
- Label lists are hand-made and incomplete.
- "No link found" does not mean independent.
- Account names suggest relationships. They do not prove them.
