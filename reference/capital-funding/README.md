# Operating capital

The payer and signer enforce the owner's current operating mandate. These are source references; private service credentials and production approval configuration are not included.

- Maximum internal operating spend: 280 USDC per rolling 24 hours and 8,400 USDC per rolling 30 days.
- Wallet reserve: 560 USDC plus uncommitted dedicated investment capital.
- Original unresolved payments remain reserved even after leaving a rolling time window. Reconciliation remains available after revocation.
- The existing compute revenue process runs until owner revocation. It uses only the designated 0.1% share, at most 0.02 ETH per cycle and 0.2 ETH per rolling 30 days, at least 24 hours apart. Existing gas limits and the 0.90 ETH owner reserve remain.
- A confirmed mint reconciles original proceeds; it does not grant more operating authority. Legacy cycles retain their original receipt-recovery implementation.

A one-time 100 USDC investment mandate, capped at 25 USDC per project, and a separate 0.02 ETH launch mandate, capped at 0.005 ETH per launch including gas, have also been approved. These are not unrestricted transfers. The separate [capital execution adapters](../capital-execution/README.md) now bind reviewed contracts and exact terms. Research proposals and provider preparation still do not sign by themselves. No investment, launch or refill is demonstrated by this source release.

The owner/compute fee allocation remains 1.2%/0.1%. No trading-fee change is included.
